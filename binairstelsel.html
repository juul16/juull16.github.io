Input – Processing – Output

Computers work in three main steps: input, processing and output. Each step has an important role in turning information into a result.

⌨️

1. Input

Input is the information a computer receives. For example, you can type on a keyboard, click a mouse or use a microphone to give information to a computer.

⚙️

2. Processing

Processing is when the computer works with the information. The CPU follows instructions and processes the data to decide what should happen next.

🖥️

3. Output

Output is the result the computer gives back. This could be text or pictures on a screen, sound from speakers or a document printed on paper.

Example: Typing on a computer

Input: You press a key on your keyboard.
Processing: The computer processes which key you pressed.
Output: The letter appears on your screen.

Color Models

Color models explain how computers represent and display colors. Different color models are used for screens, printing and digital design.

🔴

RGB

RGB stands for Red, Green and Blue. Screens combine different amounts of these three colors to create many other colors. When all three are at full intensity, the result is white.

🖨️

CMYK

CMYK stands for Cyan, Magenta, Yellow and Key (Black). It is mainly used for printing. Printers mix these inks in different amounts to create colors on paper.

🎨

Hexadecimal Colors

Hexadecimal color codes are used in websites and digital design. A code starts with a hashtag and uses six letters or numbers to represent a color. For example, #FF0000 represents red.
